# QuestAndReward Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

QuestAndReward stores your quests, rewards, wish goals, coin history and app preferences locally on your device. You do not need to create a QuestAndReward account. The current app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or an automatic developer-operated synchronization service.

## Purchases

On iOS, Apple processes subscription payments. The app uses StoreKit to read and verify your subscription entitlement, including expiration and revocation, so it can enable Premium features. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's processing is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Backups and sharing

You can export a JSON backup that includes your app data and history. On iOS, exported files are stored in the app's Documents folder, which is accessible through Files. You control whether to copy or share a backup with other people or storage providers. Their privacy policies apply to any copies you give them. Keep backups private; they may contain personal information you entered in task or reward names.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until the app's storage is deleted. Deleting an individual task or reward preserves historical completion and accounting records. Reset restores the visible initial app state but retains archived records and the local audit history; it is not permanent erasure of all stored data. It also does not remove backup files you have exported. For full local removal, delete the app (not just offload it), and delete exported copies in Files or wherever you saved them. Device or cloud backups managed by your operating system are controlled by your system settings. Uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

## External links and diagnostics

Opening a policy, support or terms link uses your browser and contacts the website you selected. GitHub hosts this policy and our public issue tracker; [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies there. Apple may process diagnostics according to your device's analytics and sharing settings. Do not attach private backups, purchase receipts or account credentials to public issues.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the QuestAndReward maintainer through the [project's issue tracker](https://github.com/Phanstal/QuestAndReward/issues), without including sensitive personal information. Changes to the app's data handling will be reflected in this policy before release.
